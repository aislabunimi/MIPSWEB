Use cases of the exam: read two integers a and b, print "The product is <a*b>".
Lines before the first section are comments.

[case positive numbers]
[input]
5
3
[output]
The product is 15

[case negative first]
[input]
-4
6
[output]
The product is -24

[case zero]
[input]
0
9
[output]
The product is 0

[case negative second]
[input]
12
-12
[output]
The product is -144
